God forgave me faster than I forgave myself.

I could accept that He was merciful. I had a harder time letting me off the hook. I'd lie there running the old tape: the nights I couldn't remember, the people I'd let down, the man I used to be. As if staying ashamed was how I proved I was serious.

It wasn't. It was just another way to stay stuck.

I couldn't forgive myself. I'd wasted years drinking. I spent a long time building a business, only to piss it all away. I lost time with my family that I can never get back. But worrying about the past is just wasting more time. Learn from it, then dwell and delight in the Lord and in today. Don't worry about tomorrow. God is right here. That's when happiness and joy start to show up.

I used to think forgiveness was a favor someone did for me. It's the opposite. I can't control whether other people forgive me. I hope and pray they do, for their sake, not mine, because the past is where misery lives. Forgiveness is what lets a person move into the present.

Something true to hold onto: "If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just and will forgive us our sins and purify us from all unrighteousness." (1 John 1:9, NIV)

"Forget the former things; do not dwell on the past. See, I am doing a new thing! Now it springs up; do you not perceive it? I am making a way in the wilderness and streams in the wasteland." (Isaiah 43:18-19, NIV)

Confess. That's the honest part. I'm not talking about pretending the damage didn't happen. Name it. Bring it to Him. Then notice what He does with it. He forgives. He purifies. He doesn't say, "Sit in this for a few more years so I know you mean it."

The beast loves a long prosecution. It will use your own conscience against you. "See? Look at what you did. You don't get to be free." But if God has already closed the case, you don't have to keep retrying it every night.

Putting things right still matters. Showing up still matters. But carrying the shame as a life sentence is not the same as being honest. One leads you forward. The other just chains you to yesterday.

He is doing a new thing. You don't have to wait until you feel worthy of it to take the next step.

Today's step: In your Set Free note, write one thing you've already brought to God but still haven't forgiven yourself for. Under it, write: "If He has forgiven this, I don't have to keep carrying it." Then say that second line out loud once.
