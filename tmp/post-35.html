God gave me a Bible a long time ago. A confirmation Bible. I kept it nearby for years and never really opened it. It just sat there, waiting.

I always had it close. I didn't think I could understand what was in it. It seemed like homework and a fairy tale. Those pages are so thin you can see right through them. No one actually reads the Bible, I thought.

Then one sober night, I finally picked it up. These days it's filling up with tabs, bookmarks, and notes. The thing that had been sitting in the same place all those years was suddenly alive. God had left it there for me. I was the one who hadn't turned the page.

I never knew where to start. I had tried reading Genesis and got through it. Then I figured I'd go on to Mark, Luke, and John. And it turns out a guy like me can understand it. Turns out a guy like me can be completely changed in a matter of a couple of days.

Something true to hold onto: "Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path." (Psalm 119:105, NIV)

"As the rain and the snow come down from heaven, and do not return to it without watering the earth and making it bud and flourish, so that it yields seed for the sower and bread for the eater, so is my word that goes out from my mouth: It will not return to me empty, but will accomplish what I desire and achieve the purpose for which I sent it." (Isaiah 55:10-11, NIV)

A lamp for my feet is not a floodlight for the whole road. It's enough light for the next step. That's all I needed, and it's all I need now.

His word doesn't come back empty. Even when I left it shut. Even when I was, as I wrote last week, flipping Him off with both hands. He still didn't take it back. He let it wait.

If you've got a Bible that's been sitting closed, you don't have to catch up. You don't have to know where to start. Open it. Ask Him to make it a lamp for the step in front of you. Keep knocking. He has not been far.
