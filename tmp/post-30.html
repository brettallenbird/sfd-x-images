We started with tomorrow. The word that let me put my whole life somewhere I never had to live it.

Now we're here. Thirty mornings of telling the truth, bringing things into the light, and trying one small step. Maybe it took you thirty days. Maybe it took longer. You didn't owe these mornings a perfect attendance record.

And getting to Day 30 doesn't mean you've had thirty sober days. If you haven't, I'm still glad you're here. Reading this isn't a test you failed because your life is still messy.

I thought my sin was going to completely consume me, that I would die in it. One night my imagination was running wild, and I saw God's hand reaching down into a lake of fire, pulling me out with one hand and placing me on the shore. Once I was on the shore, all I had to do was start in the smallest way possible. I didn't have to be perfect. I just needed to start and keep God close. The rest is history.

If I could say one thing to the man on the couch, it would be this: Tomorrow is here. It's always here. Ride out today. God has you, and He's right there with you. You'll make it. Keep asking. Keep knocking. Let Him in. He loves you.

Something true to hold onto: "being confident of this, that he who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus." (Philippians 1:6, NIV)

"Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, the new creation has come: The old has gone, the new is here!" (2 Corinthians 5:17, NIV)

He began it. He carries it on. That's a good place to put the weight I used to pile on my own shoulders.

I'm still rebuilding. I haven't reached some place where nothing hurts and every consequence has disappeared. Freedom isn't a magic undo. It's getting to live the next part without the bottle making all the decisions.

Your Set Free note holds a little evidence now. A truth you finally named. Something you're grateful for. A promise you kept. Maybe a prayer you didn't think you'd ever pray. Don't throw that away because the beast points out what isn't finished.

There's still a gap between where you are and where you want to be. You don't have to cross the whole thing before breakfast. Keep knocking. Take the next honest step.
