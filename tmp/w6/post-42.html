In a few weeks, a lot of people are going to try Dry January. I'm glad. Any month without the bottle is a good month.

But I want to say something to the person who's already planning it. The person thinking, "I'll get through the holidays, and then I'll stop."

I know that voice. It's the same word we started with on Day 1. Tomorrow. Monday. After the holidays. January. It sounds like a plan, but for me it was just a nicer way of saying not yet. Tomorrow was the place I put my whole life so I never had to live it.

Remember the grandpa who put out his cigarette at dinner and said that's it? He didn't wait for the first of the month. A made up mind doesn't need a calendar.

Today is also the first Sunday of Advent, the season of waiting for Christ to come. That's a good kind of waiting. It's waiting with your eyes open and your heart ready. Putting off freedom until January isn't that. It's the beast buying itself five more weeks.

Something true to hold onto: "For he says, 'In the time of my favor I heard you, and in the day of salvation I helped you.' I tell you, now is the time of God's favor, now is the day of salvation." (2 Corinthians 6:2, NIV)

"The LORD has done it this very day; let us rejoice today and be glad." (Psalm 118:24, NIV)

Now is the time. Not because you have to get it perfect before Christmas. Because God's favor is already here, today, and you don't have to wait for a new year to receive it. What a gift from God this could be!

You can still do Dry January. Just don't make it your starting line. Make it something you walk into already moving.
