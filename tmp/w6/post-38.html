Tonight is one of the biggest drinking nights of the year. Everybody's home for the holiday. The old crowd is back at the old bar. Tomorrow's a day off, so tonight feels like a free pass.

I used to go out and get absolutely smashed and then wake up just to drink it off before hosting a party. I don't remember a lot of my life during holidays.

I know about free passes. Football was mine. Pregame, in game, postgame, and Thanksgiving is a football day. Holidays come with a built-in excuse, and the beast knows every one of them by heart. "It's the holiday." "Everybody's out."

But tomorrow is not a day for sleeping anything off. Tomorrow is a table, and people you love, and a meal you actually get to taste. Tonight is when you protect that.

The time to decide is before the night starts, not after someone hands you a drink. You might stay in. That's always allowed, and it isn't hiding. It's choosing tomorrow over tonight. If you want to see old friends, go early, drive yourself, and pick your exit time before you walk in. You can leave whenever you want.

Something true to hold onto: "In peace I will lie down and sleep, for you alone, LORD, make me dwell in safety." (Psalm 4:8, NIV)

"Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest." (Matthew 11:28, NIV)

I spent a lot of nights passing out and calling it sleep. That's not rest. Rest is lying down in peace, with nothing to hide, nothing to piece together in the morning, and nobody to apologize to. That kind of sleep is a gift I never got from a bottle.

If you're tired tonight, tired of the fight, tired of the holidays coming at you, Jesus doesn't ask you to earn your rest first. He says come. Bring it to Him tonight, and let Him carry the weight while you sleep.

You don't have to win the whole holiday season tonight. You just have to get to tomorrow morning clear.
